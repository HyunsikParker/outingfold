03 Check the whole source

A matching quote can still leave something important out. Review every line, including unchecked ones. Change a section or add a missed excerpt.

The card repeats your source. It does not check current conditions or plan a route. Recheck hours, closures, weather and access before leaving.

A small job for a local model.

Gemma reads the notes and chooses source IDs for three sections. OutingFold checks those IDs and copies the original words. The model cannot add a new sentence to your card.

You can see and change the selection before saving. The offline HTML includes every source line, even those left off the folded page. The paper has room for something you notice yourself.

No accounts, analytics scripts or location requests. The app keeps notes in memory; download them to keep a copy. Local mode sends them only to the local Node server and your local Ollama instance.
